# TSK-330 — StopPlacementAdvisor + PositionSizingCalculator

## Obiettivo
Implementare in pure-function Kotlin lo stop suggerito (3 candidati prioritari), il position sizing 2%/6% Rule e il reward/risk vs DCF intrinsic value, inclusi nel `TechnicalAnalysisResponse`. Nessuna persistenza dell'equity.

## Scope

### StopPlacementAdvisor (`stopSuggestion`)
- 3 candidati in priorità: `SUPPORT_BASED` (sotto `levels.support[0]`, buffer 0.5%, Murphy §Page 82) > `SMA200_BASED` (sotto SMA200, buffer 0.5%, solo se prezzo > SMA200) > `ATR_BASED` (`currentPrice − 2 × ATR14`).
- Ritorna il primo applicabile; se nessuno → `NOT_CALCULABLE` con rationale esplicito.
- Output: `type`, `stopPrice`, `stopDistance`, `stopDistancePct`, `anchorReference`, `rationale`.

### PositionSizingCalculator (`positionSizing`)
- 2% Rule (Elder §50): `maxRisk = equity × 0.02`, `shares = floor(maxRisk / stopDistance)`, `positionValue = shares × currentPrice`, `positionPctEquity`. Quando `positionValue > equity` → cap shares a `equity/currentPrice` e `warning = POSITION_EXCEEDS_EQUITY`.
- 6% Rule: `maxAggregateRiskPerMonth = 0.06 × equity` + disclaimer testuale; il BE NON traccia altre posizioni dell'utente.
- `equity` da query param `?equity=...` (default 50000), MAI persistito server-side (ADR-030 §4).

### rewardRiskRatio
- `upside = dcfIntrinsicValue − currentPrice` (DCF letto da `/api/analysis/{ticker}`, EP-007), `downside = stopDistance`, `rewardRiskRatio = upside/downside`.
- Etichette: `EXCELLENT ≥3 | ACCEPTABLE ≥2 | MARGINAL ≥1 | UNFAVORABLE <1 | NOT_APPLICABLE` (dcf assente o ≤ currentPrice).

### Vincoli
- Tutto pure-function Kotlin, no LLM. Output inclusi nel `TechnicalAnalysisResponse`. Kdoc cita [[ta-stop-placement-position-sizing]] e [[elder-risk-management-2pct-6pct]].

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `stopSuggestion` con `type` corretto per support/SMA200/ATR secondo priorità; `NOT_CALCULABLE` su edge case con rationale.
- [ ] Buffer 0.5% applicato a SUPPORT_BASED e SMA200_BASED.
- [ ] `positionSizing.twoPercentRule` calcola shares/positionValue/positionPctEquity con formula Elder e `equity` da query param.
- [ ] `warning = POSITION_EXCEEDS_EQUITY` quando positionValue > equity.
- [ ] `sixPercentRule.maxAggregateRiskPerMonth = 0.06 × equity` con disclaimer.
- [ ] `rewardRiskRatio` con etichette `EXCELLENT/ACCEPTABLE/MARGINAL/UNFAVORABLE/NOT_APPLICABLE` vs DCF.
- [ ] Equity mai persistita server-side.
- [ ] Output pure-function, nessuna chiamata LLM, inclusi nel `TechnicalAnalysisResponse`.
