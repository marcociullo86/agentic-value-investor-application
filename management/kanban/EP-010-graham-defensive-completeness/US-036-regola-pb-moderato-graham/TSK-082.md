1. Creare `PbLatestRuleTest.kt` in `./src/backend/src/test/kotlin/com/valueinvesting/rules/`.
2. Test unitari:
   - **Verde**: `price = 12, bvps = 10.0` → `pb = 1.2` → `GREEN`.
   - **Giallo**: `price = 25, bvps = 10.0` → `pb = 2.5` → `YELLOW`.
