## News, Dividends, Earnings & Analyst Estimates

### Earnings, Dividends, Splits APIs
Access historical and upcoming corporate actions.
**Parameters:**
- `symbol`* (string), `from` (date), `to` (date)

### Stock News API
Fetch the latest news articles related to specific tickers.
**Parameters:**
- `tickers` (string), `limit` (number)

### Analyst Estimates & Upgrades/Downgrades
Access Wall Street analyst expectations and ratings.
**Parameters:**
- `symbol`* (string)

### SEC Filings API
Direct links and parsed data for forms like 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K.
**Parameters:**
- `symbol`* (string), `type` (string)

### ESG API
Environmental, Social, and Governance scoring data.
**Parameters:**
- `symbol`* (string)
