## Company Executives & Compensation

### Company Executives API [Globe Flag]
Retrieve detailed information on company executives (name, title, gender, year of birth).
**Parameters:**
- `symbol`* (string)
- `active` (string): true

### Executive Compensation API [USA Flag]
Retrieve comprehensive compensation data (salaries, stock awards).
**Parameters:**
- `symbol`* (string)

### Executive Compensation Benchmark API [USA Flag]
Compare executive pay against peers.
**Parameters:**
- `year` (number)
